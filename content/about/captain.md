## About the captain

**Jeff Keen** has spent his life fishing the waters off Holland on Lake Michigan. With over **20 years of experience** in the charter fishing industry, Jeff brings a deep knowledge of the lake and a passion for helping others enjoy it. He began his career as a first mate for several local captains in the Holland and Saugatuck areas and has been running charters as a captain out of Holland since 2013.

Jeff’s approach to charter fishing is rooted in preparation, safety, and creating a great experience for every guest. His commitment includes:

- Constantly monitoring weather and fishing conditions
- Staying up-to-date on the latest techniques and productive baits
- Sharing his passion for fishing and boating with guests of all ages
- Maintaining certifications in First Aid and CPR
- Being fully licensed and inspected by the U.S. Coast Guard

{{< figure src="/about/family.jpg" title="Captain & Crew" alt="Captain Jeff with his three daughters">}}

Jeff has been married for **22 years** and values time with his family as much as time on the water. During the off-season, he serves as an **assistant principal at a local high school**, where he enjoys working with students and building relationships. His background in education, combined with his love for the outdoors, makes each trip both engaging and memorable.

Whether you’re a seasoned angler or heading out for your first time, Captain Jeff is committed to making your experience on Lake Michigan one you won’t forget.
